## Our story

It all began on **27th July 2025**, when two strangers met for the very first time, knowing little about each other and even less about the beautiful journey that lay ahead. While our families began their conversations, we were given some time in a room to talk and get to know each other. We started with a simple “Hello,” shared a little about ourselves, and spent around half an hour talking before heading back to our respective homes. Little did we know that this simple meeting was the beginning of something special.

A few days later, our families gave us the chance to spend some more time together, just the two of us. Over dinner at a restaurant, we talked about our lives, our families, our friends, and the dreams we hoped to fulfil. We even took a little walk around the restaurant, finding comfort in conversations that seemed to flow naturally.

Somewhere between those conversations and shared smiles, we felt a quiet certainty that maybe, this was going to be something special. There was no grand movie moment, just a simple feeling that we could be good together.

As more meetings followed and our families grew closer, that little feeling turned into something we both wanted to hold on to. And on **24th August 2025**, we made it official, choosing to begin our journey together.

**And now, we are ready to tie the knot!**&#x20;

Some connections feel as though they were always meant to be. Ours is a story of two hearts finding a home in each other, two families coming together, and a beautiful new chapter waiting to unfold.

As we take this next step towards forever, we want our favourite people beside us. Your love, laughter, blessings, and presence will make our special day even more meaningful.

We cannot wait to celebrate the beginning of our forever with you!
